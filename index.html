Your records are yours

Rokda has no server. We never see your money, your entries or your name, because none of it ever reaches us.

  • No account, no sign-up

    Open the app and start writing. There is nothing to register and no password to remember for us.

  • Works offline

    Every screen works without internet. Only a Google Drive backup needs a connection, and only when you ask for one.

  • Encrypted on your phone

    Your records and receipt photos are stored encrypted. A PIN or your fingerprint keeps them closed to anyone who picks up your phone.

  • No ads, no tracking

    Rokda contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Back up to your own cloud

Lose your phone, keep your records. Every backup is locked with a password only you know before it leaves the phone.

  1. Any cloud

    Save a backup file

    Tap Back up now and save one encrypted file to Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, email, or a computer. Your choice.

  2. Google Drive

    Connect your Drive

    Sign in with your own Google account. Rokda makes one folder, Rokda Backups, and can see nothing else in your Drive.

  3. Automatic

    Daily auto-backup

    Turn it on and Rokda backs up to your Drive once a day, on Wi-Fi only, when you open the app.

Keep your backup password safe. Without it, nobody can open your backup, not Google and not us. That is what keeps it private, and it is also why we cannot reset it for you.

Questions

Is Rokda free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid features.

Does Rokda read my SMS or connect to my bank?

No. You write your entries yourself. Rokda never asks for SMS access or bank logins.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Install Rokda on your new phone, then restore from your backup file or connect the same Google account. Type your backup password and everything comes back, including receipts.

Can Rokda see my other Google Drive files?

No. Rokda asks Google only for access to files it creates itself. It cannot see, open or change anything else in your Drive.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete all data. To remove Drive backups, delete the Rokda Backups folder in your Google Drive. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on the phone.
