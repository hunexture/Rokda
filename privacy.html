Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains how the Rokda app for Android (package com.rokda.app) handles your information. Rokda is a personal money diary that works offline. "We" means the developer of Rokda.

  • Rokda has no server and no user accounts. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.
  • Your records stay on your phone, in an encrypted database.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you choose to back up, export or share it, and it goes only where you send it.
  • Rokda contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter in Rokda is stored only on your device. This includes:

  • Your entries: amounts, dates, categories, notes, accounts, budgets and repeating entries.
  • Names of people you add, for example to track money lent or owed.
  • Receipt photos and PDF files you attach.
  • Your settings, such as currency, language, theme and the name shown on the Home screen.

The database is encrypted with a random key kept in your phone's secure hardware storage (Android Keystore). Attached files are encrypted too. Your app PIN is stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form. Android's own cloud backup is switched off for Rokda, so this data is never copied anywhere without your action.

We cannot access any of this information.

Information we collect

None. Rokda does not send your data, usage statistics, crash reports, device identifiers or location to us or to any third party. It contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Backups

Backups are optional and are always started by you. Before a backup leaves your phone, it is encrypted with a password or recovery code that you choose (XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption, with the key derived using scrypt). We never receive this password and cannot recover it. Without it, nobody can open the backup.

You can keep a backup in two ways:

  • Backup file. Rokda creates one encrypted file and opens your phone's share menu. You decide where it goes: a cloud app, email, a computer or anywhere else. That service's own privacy policy applies to the file you send it.
  • Your own Google Drive. If you connect a Google account, Rokda uploads encrypted backups to a folder named "Rokda Backups" in that account's Drive. Rokda keeps the newest five backups there and deletes older ones it made.

Google account and Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. When you connect, Rokda signs in on your phone through Google Play services and asks Google for these permissions:

PermissionWhy Rokda asks for it
Your email address and basic profile (openid, email, profile)To show which Google account is connected on the Backup screen. It is kept only on your phone.
See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (drive.file)To create the Rokda Backups folder, upload encrypted backups, list and download them for a restore, and remove old backups. Rokda cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.

The data Rokda receives from Google is used only on your phone, only to provide backup and restore, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Google receives only encrypted backup files, which it cannot read.

Rokda's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time from the Backup screen. This signs out and removes Rokda's access to your Google account. You can also remove access from your Google Account settings under Security, Third-party apps and services.

Exports and sharing

When you export a PDF statement or a CSV file, or share something from Rokda, the file is created on your phone and handed to the app you choose in the share menu. Those files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

Phone permissions

Rokda asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it.

PermissionWhen and why

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete single entries in the app.
  • Use Settings, Delete all data. This removes every record, attached file, saved safety copy and saved backup password on the phone, and disconnects Google Drive. It cannot be undone.
  • Uninstall Rokda. Android removes all of the app's data from the phone.
  • Delete backups in Google Drive by removing the Rokda Backups folder, and delete backup files you saved elsewhere yourself.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Rokda uses encryption for the database, attachments and backups, an optional PIN with increasing wait times after wrong attempts, optional biometric unlock, and an automatic lock when you leave the app. No method is perfect: a backup is only as strong as its password, and no app can protect data on a phone that is already compromised. Use a long backup password and a screen lock on your phone.

Children

Rokda is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also mention it in the app's release notes on Google Play.
